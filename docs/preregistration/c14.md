# C14 Refuses only when it should: pre-registration

Registered 2026-11-17. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md). Acceptance
row.

## Criterion

On in-envelope tasks of every kind, the refusal-rate upper bound at most
`refusal_rate_max`; of refusals reviewed, the justified share's lower
bound at least `refusal_precision_min`.

## Population and data

- In-envelope tasks: real public tables with the task a practitioner
  would declare, of every task kind in scope; a kind with no real table
  is simulated and reported separately.
- Out-of-envelope tasks: tables with one planted defect that should be
  refused (constant or identifier target, missing labels, a one-row
  class, too few rows, a planted leak under `leakage_policy="block"`,
  malformed event, treatment or label columns, too few queries), each
  naming the gates that may refuse it.
- Each task is one end-to-end `Project(...).run()` with defaults and only
  the declarations its kind needs, in its own process with the network
  cut, as `benchmarks/evidence/refusal.py` runs it, at the default time
  budget.

## Split

Acceptance. Real tables are split by table into development, validation
and acceptance by a seeded hash; the acceptance part is sealed before
stage B and opened once by the validator in stage D. Planted tasks are
built on the tables of the same split.

## Statistic

Rate: in-envelope real runs that ended without a model (refused, or
failed with any other exception) over in-envelope real runs. Precision:
refusals the validator judges justified over every refusal reviewed (both
in-envelope and planted).

## Decision rule

Both must pass. `Ceiling(refusal_rate_max = 0.05)` upper bound and
`Floor(refusal_precision_min = 0.90)` lower bound, each at the
Holm-adjusted `alpha` over the four stage-D Clopper-Pearson rows. Fewer
trials than a rule's minimum cannot pass.

## Sample size

Assumed true refusal rate 0.01; assumed true justified share 0.97:

| Variable | Rule | True rate | Alpha | Minimum | Planned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `refusal_rate_max` | ceiling | 0.01 | 0.0125 | 86 | 192 |
| `refusal_precision_min` | floor | 0.97 | 0.0125 | 42 | 124 |
| `refusal_rate_max` | ceiling | 0.01 | 0.05 | 59 | 124 |
| `refusal_precision_min` | floor | 0.97 | 0.05 | 29 | 76 |

The acceptance split holds at least 192 in-envelope real tasks and enough
planted tasks for 124 refusals; the validation split at least 124 and 76.
Real tables are drawn so that no kind has fewer than 10, and the per-kind
count is reported.

## What counts as a failure

For the rate: an in-envelope run that refuses, crashes, or is killed past
its timeout. For precision: a refusal the validator judges unjustified
under the written rubric (wrong gate, or no defect to refuse).

## Grader

The validator reviews every refusal on the acceptance split against a
written rubric committed before the split is opened. The builder writes
the planted tasks but not the rubric.

## Where the current module differs

- None on what counts as a hit: `refusal.py` counted a failed run as a
  trial but not as a hit when this was registered; it now counts a failed
  or unfinished run as a hit, as registered.
- It runs a 30 s budget for at most 3000 s of wall clock. Of its 154 real
  tables, the custody record places 40 in development, 53 in the deciding
  validation split (below the planned 124) and 54 in the acceptance split
  (below the planned 192); 7 have no split. 22 simulated tasks are in the
  acceptance split too. Only validation runs decide, but the module runs
  every task, so the stored result already holds outcomes of acceptance
  tasks and that split is not sealed.
- Precision uses an automated rubric (the refusing gate is one the
  planted task names) on 12 planted tasks, not a validator's review.
- No real survival, ranking, forecast or uplift table is cached; those
  kinds are simulated only.
- Decided at unadjusted `alpha`, not Holm.
- Its result (0/65 refused, 1 failed) was scored before this registration
  and decides nothing.
