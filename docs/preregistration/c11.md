# C11 A non-specialist uses it correctly: pre-registration

Registered 2026-11-17. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Criterion

`non_specialists` = 5 non-specialists each finish 3 problems from raw
files to a served model within `user_task_minutes_max` = 60 minutes; at
least `comprehension_min` of their readings of a result are correct.

## Population and data

Five people who have not trained a model professionally, recruited by the
owner; 15 problems (raw files plus a written question), drawn from a pool
written before recruitment, each person given 3 at random, balanced by
data kind. After each problem the person answers a fixed set of
comprehension questions about the result (what the metric means, whether
the interval supports the claim, what the refusal or warning says).

## Split

None applies: the sessions are run once in stage D. The problems and
questions are a fresh draw from the sealed pool; pilot sessions (if any)
use other problems and are development.

## Statistic

Problems finished within 60 minutes, out of 15; correct answers over
answers.

## Decision rule

- Completion: policy, all 15 problems finished within 60 minutes.
- Comprehension: `Floor(comprehension_min = 0.90)` lower bound at
  `alpha`.

## Sample size

Assumed true comprehension 0.97:

| Variable | Rule | True rate | Alpha | Minimum | Planned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `comprehension_min` | floor | 0.97 | 0.05 | 29 | 76 |

76 answers over 5 people is 16 questions per person (80 answers), about 5
per problem. Answers are clustered by person; the binomial bound treats
them as independent, so the per-person share is reported beside the
verdict and any person below 0.75 is named.

## What counts as a failure

A problem not finished in 60 minutes, or finished only with help from the
observer; a wrong or "don't know" answer.

## Grader

The answer key is written with the questions, before the sessions, by
someone other than the builder; observed sessions are arranged by the
owner and scored against the key by someone who did not build the
product.

## Where the current module differs

There is no evidence module. `benchmarks/scorecard.py` reports the row as
"not measured".
