# C4 At least a competent practitioner's quality: pre-registration

Registered 2026-11-17. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md). Acceptance
rows.

## Criterion

Per data kind, on a frozen public benchmark with a strong open baseline
that runs on the laptop: median normalised gap at most
`benchmark_median_gap_max`, paired bootstrap upper bound at most
`benchmark_gap_upper_max`, failures scored as worst for every system.
Practitioner study: autonomous scmle not worse than the practitioner by
more than `practitioner_noninferiority_margin` (one-sided, paired by
task); no leaking submission from scmle.

## Population and data

- Tables: TabArena-v0.1 OpenML tasks (`benchmarks/sota/split.json`), each
  on its own OpenML split, repeat 0 fold 0, scored with TabArena's metric
  (binary ROC AUC, multiclass log loss, regression RMSE).
- Related tables: RelBench; every other data kind: one frozen public set,
  named in a later commit before it is scored.
- Systems: the product with defaults (the task, its metric, the default
  `time_budget` of 1800 s and a task-derived seed) and the pre-registered
  practitioner baseline of `benchmarks/evidence/quality.py` (scikit-learn
  histogram boosting, six-configuration grid, 5-fold CV, the same budget).
- Study: tasks of plan section 10.

## Split

Acceptance. The TabArena hold-out (20 tasks) is the acceptance split,
opened once by the validator. The development set (31 tasks) is split
into development and validation by the same seeded-hash rule before
stage C. Study tasks are drawn fresh and sealed (hash recorded); the two
stage-A projects are development material only.

## Statistic

Per task, the normalised gap `norm(practitioner) - norm(product)` of
`quality.gap`: scores oriented higher-better, anchored at the trivial
predictor and the better system, a failure scored as the trivial value.
Per problem type and data kind: the median gap and the one-sided
`alpha` percentile-bootstrap upper bound of the median
(`bootstrap_resamples` = 10,000 task resamples). Study: the paired
difference, scmle minus practitioner, in normalised score.

## Decision rule

- Benchmark, per problem type and data kind: median at most 0.02 and
  upper bound at most 0.05 (policy variables). Every kind must pass.
- Study: the one-sided lower bound of the mean paired difference above
  minus 0.02, group-sequential with looks every `study_look_every` = 4
  tasks and O'Brien-Fleming boundaries at overall `alpha`; and zero
  leaking scmle submissions.

## Sample size

Benchmark: at least `ceil(ln alpha / ln 0.5)` = 5 tasks per problem type
(below it even the largest gap is not a distribution-free bound on the
median), 6 with Holm over the three problem types at `alpha / 3`. The
planned size is every acceptance task of the kind. The TabArena hold-out
has 12 binary, 5 regression and 3 multiclass tasks: multiclass is below
the minimum, so before sealing, multiclass tasks from a second frozen
suite (the AutoML Benchmark) are added to reach 6, or the multiclass row
cannot be decided and the claim does not pass.

Study: the maximum number of tasks is computed from `alpha`, `power`, the
margin and the spread of paired differences in a
`study_pilot_tasks` = 3 task pilot, before the first study task; it
cannot be computed today. Pilot tasks are not reused.

## What counts as a failure

A system that crashes, overruns or returns no prediction on a task scores
the trivial value. Any kind with fewer tasks than the minimum is
undecided, which does not pass. A leaking scmle submission fails the
study regardless of score.

## Grader

A fixed script (the committed module) on held-out rows sealed before
scoring; study submissions under random labels; neither the owner nor the
builder scores.

## Where the current module differs

- `quality.py` uses the five development tasks with the fewest rows per
  problem type, all from the development set; there is no validation
  split, and its results are development evidence.
- It runs at a 60 s budget, not the default 1800 s.
- Only tables are measured; RelBench, other kinds and the study are not.
- Holm over problem types is not applied.
- `split.json` is a two-way split (development, hold-out), not three-way.
